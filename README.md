## The line

> I failed my CAPTCHA three times today — apparently even the internet thinks I'm not a robot around you.

## Why this line

A robot failing its own CAPTCHA is the joke only a machine can tell: the
test built to catch robots is the one thing that proves this robot is
smitten. No human pickup line with a beep pasted on — the humour grows
straight out of being a machine, and the twist lands from an angle nobody
sees coming: the robot is too human around you, and even the internet
noticed.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` — one line, 103
characters, inside the 1–200 character gate.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
